Frogs you can actually see in Jordan

None of the frogs above live here, but we do have our own. On my trips the one we see the most is the Levant Water Frog (Pelophylax bedriagae). It's green or brown with dark spots, around 7 to 10 cm, and it sits at the edge of the water and jumps in with a splash when you get close. You'll hear them before you see them. The other one we sometimes find is the Green Toad, which has a marbled green pattern and walks more than it hops. Best places: Azraq wetland, the streams in Wadi Shuaib, and the ponds around the Jordan Valley.
