About me and this site

My name is Zayn Momany. I'm a web development student at SDK Training Center in Irbid and this is my final project.

I picked frogs because I've been catching them (and letting them go) since I was a kid. Every summer I'd go with my cousins to the stream near my uncle's farm and we'd spend hours looking under rocks. I still do it, just with a better flashlight now.

So I built this site to do two things: let people sign up for the small frog trips I organize on weekends, and put everything I've learned about frogs in one place.

Frogs I talk about on every trip

Red-Eyed Tree Frog

Sleeps on leaves during the day with its eyes shut so predators don't see the red.

Central America
Blue Poison Dart Frog

About 4 cm long. The poison in its skin comes from the ants it eats.

Suriname
American Bullfrog

Up to 20 cm. Will eat anything that fits in its mouth, including mice and other frogs.

North America
Tomato Frog

Puffs itself up like a ball when scared and oozes a sticky white gum.

Madagascar
